# Paper Ladder

A calm daily word ladder for adults who like a daily puzzle. Change one letter at a time to get from one word to another, and every step must be a real English word.

**Paper Ladder: Daily Word Game** is free on iPhone and Android. It has no account and no login, and your games, run and stats stay on your phone.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6831459761) · [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coderdoniv.paperladder)

## How it works

1. **Start with today's puzzle.** Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, with a start word and a target word.
2. **Change one letter at a time.** Each step must be an ordinary English word. A step that is not a word is refused, with the reason shown, and the changed letter is marked on the ladder.
3. **Finish at par, then share.** Par is the shortest ladder for that day's puzzle. Share your result without spoilers: the answer words are never shown.

## The three moments

- **Today's puzzle.** The Home screen shows the day's start and target, and the ladder grows one rung at a time.
- **A step refused, a hint offered.** A word that is not allowed is turned back with a plain reason. A free first hint reveals the next word without spoiling the rest.
- **Result and streak.** Your steps are set against par, with a spoiler-free share and your streak.

## Questions

**Is it free?**
Yes. It is supported by ads: a small banner on screens outside the game, and an optional rewarded video if you want an extra hint. There are never ads while you play.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no login and no server. Your games, run and stats are stored only on your phone.

**Can I play without a connection?**
Yes. The daily puzzle is made on your phone from the date, and practice mode works offline.

**What if today's puzzle is too hard?**
Practice mode has Easy, Medium and Hard ladders, as many as you like. Each one is a fresh puzzle, so you can practise without touching your daily run.

**Which words count?**
Ordinary English words, including British spellings.

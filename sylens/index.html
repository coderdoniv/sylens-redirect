Game Modes

Choose your way to play.

Just Vibing

One-team charades, zero setup. Jump straight in and act out movie after movie. Skip freely before you reveal, and keep the streak going for as long as you like.

Squad War

Split into teams and battle it out. Every turn runs on the clock: guess fast for a time bonus, spend points on power-ups like Clue or Change Movie, and force sudden death if the scores are tied.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sylens?

Sylens is a fast-paced movie charades game. One player acts out a movie without speaking while others race the clock to guess it.

How do I play?

Pick a movie, tap to reveal it to yourself, act it out with no talking, and let your team shout out guesses before time runs out.

How many people can play?

Sylens needs at least two people: one to act and one to guess. Just Vibing is for one team, while Squad War is for two or more teams. You can also play together with TV Party.

What's the difference between Just Vibing and Squad War?

Just Vibing is casual one-team play with no scoring and unlimited skips. Squad War is a timed, team-based mode with scoring, power-ups and sudden-death tiebreakers.

Can I play Sylens on a TV?

Yes. Sylens runs natively on Android TV today through the same Google Play listing. TV Party lets phones act as controllers while the TV mirrors a Squad War game, without ever showing the movie title on screen. An Apple TV app is currently in App Store review.

What languages are supported?

Sylens supports English, Hindi, Malayalam and Tamil. Hindi, Malayalam and Tamil titles are also available fully spelled out in English letters, for players who speak the language but can't read the script.

Can players who read different languages play together?

Yes. Each player picks their own languages, separate from the app's display language, and Sylens remembers it for their turn, so a mixed-language group can play on the same team.

How does scoring work in Squad War?

Each turn starts with a point value that ticks down. Guess correctly and you keep the remaining points plus a time bonus. Power-ups like Change Movie, Clue and Half Name cost points to use, and a timeout scores zero.

Tips & Tricks

Master the Sylens.

Act the genre first

A quick horror flinch or a dance move narrows things down fast, lead with the vibe before the details.

Watch the clock

In Squad War, your score is tied to time remaining: a fast, confident guess earns a bigger bonus than a slow one.

Spend power-ups wisely

Clue and Half Name both cost points, so save them for the movies you're genuinely stuck on.
